DocuKit Privacy Policy

App: DocuKit: PDF & Image Tools · Publisher: NPM Tech Solutions · Effective date: 1 November 2026

In short
  • Your documents and photos are processed only on your device. DocuKit never uploads them.
  • DocuKit has no accounts and doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • DocuKit shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • You can change your ad choices in the app, or remove ads with a one-time purchase.

1. Who we are

DocuKit ("the app") is published by NPM Tech Solutions ("we", "us"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com.

2. Your files

DocuKit's tools (compress, resize, images to PDF, merge, organize and reduce PDF size) work entirely on your device.

  • The files you choose are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. We never see your files, their contents or their names.
  • The app only reads the specific photos and files you pick in your device's own picker. It doesn't browse your storage or photo library.
  • Your original files are never changed. The app always creates a new file.
  • The app keeps a copy of each result on your device for the period you choose in Settings (7 days by default), so you can save or share it again. You can delete these copies at any time in Settings → Storage used, and they are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • The app keeps a list of recent jobs on your device (tool used, file names, sizes, date). It stays on your device, and you can clear it in Settings → Clear history.
  • When you choose Save, Share or Open with, the file goes only to the place or app you pick.

3. Information we collect

We, NPM Tech Solutions, do not collect personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login and no server of our own, and it doesn't send your files, file names or usage of the tools to us.

Your app settings (for example theme and default options) and the on-device counters used to limit how often ads appear are stored only on your device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DocuKit is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

DataPurpose
IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) locationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (for example, ad views and taps) and app launchesAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostic information about the app's and SDK's performanceAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set IDAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit. The ads SDK has no access to your files, file names or results, and DocuKit doesn't pass any information about your documents or how you use the tools to the ads SDK.

Google's use of this information is governed by Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. You can learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads; on iPhone, DocuKit doesn't request permission to track you).
  • Remove ads: you can remove all ads with a one-time purchase in Settings → Remove ads, or turn ads off for 24 hours by choosing to watch a short ad.

5. Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play (or Apple's App Store on iPhone). We don't receive your payment details. The app stores only an on-device record that ads have been removed, so that it can restore your purchase.

6. Permissions the app uses

DocuKit doesn't request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or your whole photo library or storage.

7. Children

DocuKit is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Files, results and history stay on your device until you delete them, until the retention period you chose passes (for result copies), or until you uninstall the app.
  • We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. For data collected by Google for ads, see Google's privacy policy and your device's ad settings.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to or limit its use for advertising. Because we don't collect personal data ourselves, most requests relate to ad data: use Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app and your device's ad settings, or contact Google. You can also contact us at npmtechsolutions@gmail.com and we'll help.

10. Security

Your files are processed inside the app's private storage on your device, which other apps can't read. Copies of results are deleted automatically after the period you choose.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when new features are added, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
